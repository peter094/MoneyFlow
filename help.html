Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

?

How do I add income or spending?

Open the Income or Spending page from MoneyFlow and tap its Add button. Enter the amount and other details, then save your entry.

?

How do I track my bills and payments?

Open Payments to add a bill or payment, including its amount and due date. You can review your payments and mark an entry as paid when you've completed it.

?

How do I track money I borrowed or lent?

Go to the Debts page. Choose Borrowed for money you owe, or Lent for money someone owes you. Add the relevant amount and repayment details.

?

Can I set a monthly budget?

Yes. Open More, choose Budget, and enter your monthly budget amount. MoneyFlow saves your budget setting so you can monitor it from your dashboard.

?

Does MoneyFlow automatically pay or repeat my bills?

No. MoneyFlow currently helps you record and organize payments, but it does not send money or automatically create new payment entries. You remain responsible for making payments yourself.

?

Will logging out delete my financial records?

No. Logging out removes your sign-in session without intentionally deleting the financial records saved on your device. However, clearing browser or website data may remove locally stored records.

?

Why don't my records appear on another device?

Some MoneyFlow records are currently stored in your browser on the device where you created them. They are not automatically synchronized across all your devices.

?

Are automatic payment reminders available?

Not yet. You can save reminder preferences in Settings, but automatic notifications are not available in the current version of MoneyFlow.
