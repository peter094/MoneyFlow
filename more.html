Answers to common MoneyFlow questions.

How do I add income or spending?

Go to the Income or Spending screen and use its Add button. You can also use Quick Add on Home.

Where do I track money I borrowed?

Open Debts, choose Borrowed, and add the amount and repayment details.

Does logging out delete my records?

Logging out removes your sign-in details. It does not intentionally clear your saved financial records on this device.

Why might different devices show different data?

Some MoneyFlow records are currently stored locally in your browser. They may not automatically synchronize between browsers or devices.
